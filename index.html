The "Why" Behind the Solutions

I am a Data-Driven Engineer with a Master's in Business Intelligence, driven by a passion for uncovering the "why" behind complex bottlenecks to build truly scalable solutions. Currently an Engineer at Salesforce, I specialize in the Agentforce platform, where I architect sophisticated agentic workflows that enable manufacturing teams to automate high-stakes processes and work significantly smarter.

I am a creator at heart, someone whose systems thinking was shaped by Supply Chain, where every dependency matters and things break if you miss the details. That process logic turns out to work anywhere. I now expand the same rigor to Finance, HR, and Industrial Operations, turning messy, fragmented workflows into clean, AI-driven solutions. The goal has never been to just fix what's broken. It's to build what comes next.

Outside of architecting data strategies and process automation, I stay sharp through continuous learning, reading, and maintaining a disciplined lifestyle centered on fitness and mindfulness.
